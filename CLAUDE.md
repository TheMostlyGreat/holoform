## What Holoform is

Holoform is being reimagined into a **neutral-fiduciary, multi-channel communication
network**: a middle layer that models each person, stays loyal to the person it's
describing, and passes to the other side only what helps both — never what one could
use against the other. It spans channels (email first, then Slack, then more) and a
connection/trust graph, and it hands off to a human when a situation could harm
someone.

The full vision, architecture, and unresolved tensions live in
[`docs/product-brief.md`](./docs/product-brief.md). Read it before product work.

**The governing rule for every feature:** does this serve something both people
would knowingly endorse, or one person's leverage over the other? Clarity,
de-escalation, mutual understanding → build it. "Help me handle them" → refuse it.

**Repo state:** the current `src/` (TypeScript) and root Python files are a legacy
email-classification prototype — the first channel adapter, not the product. Treat
them as such until the reimagined direction is committed.
